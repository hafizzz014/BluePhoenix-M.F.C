About the club

Excellence cultivated from the first whistle.

Blue Phoenix was founded in 2025 by Hafiz Bayramov and Karim Orujov. "We wanted to build a club where football is about more than just winning games. From the beginning, we wanted our players to feel like they belonged to something and to enjoy being part of the team. Of course, we want to win, but we also want to build strong friendships and create something we can be proud of."

Today, under the leadership of Head Coach and Manager Hafiz Bayramov, we continue to work towards those goals. We have had good moments and difficult ones, but our aim has always been the same. We want to improve, stay together as a team and keep moving forward. Blue Phoenix means a lot to us, and we hope the best chapters of our story are still ahead.
