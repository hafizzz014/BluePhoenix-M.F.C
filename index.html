About the club

Excellence cultivated from the first whistle.

Founded in 2025 by Hafiz Bayramov and Karim Orujov, Blue Phoenix was created with a simple idea: to build a club where football means more than just the result. We are building unity, a family, and a place where every player can be themselves without hesitation. Our goal is to win, but more important than that, we are building a legacy — a story of our own, written together through every game, every challenge and every moment.

Led by Head Coach & Manager Hafiz Bayramov, Blue Phoenix continues to grow with the same values we were founded on: loyalty, passion, unity and ambition. We are not just teammates. We are a family. This is our club. Our identity. Our story. And our story has only just begun.
